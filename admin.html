Delivery charges

Control the free-delivery threshold and the flat delivery charge shown to customers at checkout. Orders at or above the threshold ship free; orders below it are charged the flat fee. Leave the threshold blank (or set it to 0) to charge delivery on every order, and leave the charge blank to always deliver free.
